Materials

We offer several material options depending on the strength, flexibility, temperature resistance, environmental exposure, appearance, and intended application of the part.

PLA

PLA is a reliable general-purpose material suitable for prototypes, models, display parts, educational models, fixtures, and many standard applications.

PETG

PETG provides good strength, toughness, and moisture resistance. It is suitable for functional components, brackets, housings, fixtures, prototypes, and many end-use applications.

ABS

ABS is a durable engineering thermoplastic with good toughness and improved temperature resistance. It can be used for functional components, mechanical parts, housings, fixtures, and other applications requiring increased durability.

ASA

ASA provides good mechanical properties along with excellent UV and weather resistance. It is particularly useful for outdoor parts and components exposed to sunlight or environmental conditions.

TPU

TPU is a flexible, rubber-like thermoplastic suitable for parts that need to bend, compress, cushion, or absorb impact. It can be used for grips, cushions, pads, protective components, wearable products, flexible joints, impact-absorbing structures, lattice structures, soft interfaces, ergonomic products, flexible covers, and other custom flexible components.

Additional materials may be available depending on the requirements of the project.
